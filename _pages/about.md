My name is Yangdong Huang (黄杨栋). I am currently a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at [**Xiamen University**](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/){:target="_blank"}
, supervised by [**Prof. Daoheng Sun (孙道恒)**](https://sbd.xmu.edu.cn/info/1095/3499.htm){:target="_blank"}
. I received my Bachelor of Engineering degree from [**Nanjing Agricultural University**](https://www.njau.edu.cn/){:target="_blank"} in 2024. In 2025, I was a visiting student at [**The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)**](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/){:target="_blank"}, and studied biophysics and molecular dynamics simulations.

My research focuses on:
- Flexible Electronics
- Sensor Technology
- Hydrogel
